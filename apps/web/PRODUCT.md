# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: residents and property owners in Abha and Khamis Mushait who need residential or commercial cleaning. They arrive not knowing Nuqaa Asir, browse services, get an instant-or-reviewed quote, book, track status through to completion, pay offline, and leave a review. Trust is the barrier at every step — they're deciding whether to let a stranger into their home.

Secondary: Admin/ops staff who confirm, price, schedule, and execute bookings, run the quality checklist, and manage subscriptions and commercial accounts. Their surface (the Admin dashboard) is a separate product-register context, not the focus of this document.

## Product Purpose

A two-role booking and operations platform for a cleaning company: customers browse services, request a quote, book, track status, pay offline, and leave reviews or complaints; Admin confirms, prices, schedules, and executes bookings, runs quality checklists, and manages subscriptions and commercial accounts. Success on the customer portal specifically means building enough trust that a first-time visitor, unfamiliar with the brand, completes a booking rather than bouncing to an informal alternative.

## Positioning

Transparent, trackable, quality-checked cleaning — clear upfront pricing, real-time booking status, and an admin-run quality checklist on every job, in a local market of informal, unaccountable alternatives.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book a service.
- Secondary CTA: Browse the service catalog, for visitors not ready to commit.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "A cleaning company you can actually trust in your home."
- Belief ladder: this is a real, professional operation → the cleaners themselves are trustworthy and vetted → pricing is fair and clearly stated → booking and tracking are easy.
- Proof on hand: the in-product customer review/rating system. No external testimonials, certifications, or press yet — design should leave room for proof without fabricating claims that aren't backed yet.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, meticulous, warm. The voice of a professional you'd actually let into your home — not a faceless service and not a discount marketplace.

## Anti-references

Generic gig-economy apps: discount-banner clutter, stock icons, crowded undifferentiated listings. Nuqaa Asir should never read as an on-demand marketplace racing to the bottom on price.

## Design Principles

- **Legitimacy before conversion.** Every screen should first read as a real, accountable company before it asks for a booking — the belief ladder starts here, not at price.
- **Transparency as the trust mechanism.** Pricing, booking status, and quality checks are the actual proof this brand has; keep them visible and legible rather than hidden behind vague copy or buried in later steps.
- **Warmth without clutter.** Trustworthy and meticulous, not busy — resist the discount-driven, crowded instinct of gig-economy references.
- **Brand storytelling, product clarity.** The home/marketing surface carries the brand voice; the booking wizard and account area are where that trust gets cashed in; those flows should read as effortless task completion, not a continuation of the sales pitch.
- **Bilingual by default.** Arabic is the default locale with full RTL mirroring — design RTL-first, not LTR-then-patched.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline (contrast, keyboard, screen-reader support), consistent with the AA-contrast corrections already applied to the Ant Design theme. No additional requirements beyond AA at this time.
